How I approach an automation project

1. Understand

Map the current process, tools, inputs, decisions and handoffs.

2. Design

Choose the simplest workable flow, including exceptions and human review.

3. Build & test

Connect the systems, validate data and test normal and failure scenarios.

4. Handover

Document how the workflow works and what to check when something fails.
